# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Crypto-native visitors and NEAR community members discovering a playful AI-pet concept. They arrive curious, scan the first fold quickly, and need to understand the pet's promise and primary mint path without being asked to connect a wallet.

## Product Purpose

NEARGOTCHI is a landing experience for a future AI pet on NEAR. The first release communicates the concept with a clearly mock-only frontend and directs interest toward a later mint flow.

## Brand Personality

Pixel-mechanical, mischievous, warm. It should feel like holding a cherished LCD pet device, not using a generic crypto dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid dark-neon crypto clichés, glass panels, token-price dashboards, generic SaaS hero layouts, financial promises, and anything that makes the demo look like a live wallet or purchase flow.

## Design Principles

1. Let the pixel device and pet assets carry the world-building.
2. Make the primary story readable in one glance at 1920px.
3. Keep the mock boundary honest and visible where actions later become transactional.
4. Use a strict, tactile LCD-device composition rather than generic card layouts.
5. Give copy breathing room, then let a single green action lead.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, semantic landmarks and headings, visible keyboard focus, text equivalents for informative art, and reduced motion support before motion is introduced.
